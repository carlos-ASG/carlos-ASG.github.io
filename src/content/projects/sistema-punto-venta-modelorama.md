## The Problem

Modelorama CompaBeto needed a point of sale system tailored to their specific beer retail needs. Commercial solutions failed to meet business requirements: shrinkage control, shipment management, custom reports, and offline operation.

## The Solution

**Sistema POS Modelorama** is a completely custom-built desktop application covering the full business operation cycle. From sales processing to waste control, all integrated in a standalone solution that works without internet dependency.

### Key Features

1. **Point of Sale**:
   - Fast sales processing
   - Product search by code or name
   - Automatic change and tax calculation
   - Sales receipt printing

2. **Inventory Management**:
   - Real-time stock control
   - Low stock product alerts
   - Shipment entry registration
   - Shrinkage and waste tracking

3. **Administration**:
   - Employee and permissions management
   - Sales reports by date range
   - Product profitability analysis
   - Automatic data backups

### Technology

- **Framework**: .NET Windows Forms for native Windows interface
- **Database**: Embedded SQLite for standalone serverless operation
- **Architecture**: Desktop application with local data persistence

### Result

A robust and reliable system that successfully replaced commercial alternatives that didn't fit the business. The custom solution covers 100% of the client's specific requirements and operates stably in production.
