## The Problem

Public transportation companies struggle to gather real user feedback and manage complaints effectively. Traditional processes are fragmented—paper forms, scattered emails, disconnected systems—making it impossible to analyze data or make informed decisions. Additionally, each transportation company would need its own independent infrastructure, multiplying costs and operational complexity.

## The Solution

**Tu Voz en Ruta** is a multi-tenant web platform that centralizes feedback collection and complaint management for transportation companies. Passengers simply scan a QR code on their vehicle, answer a quick survey about their experience, and can report specific issues—all within minutes.

### How It Works

1. **For Passengers**: Scan the unique QR code on any vehicle → Answer survey questions (cleanliness, operator treatment, punctuality) → Report complaints or suggestions → Submit

2. **For Administrators**: Review responses in real-time → Analyze satisfaction trends → Identify recurring problems → Export reports → Make data-driven improvements

3. **For the Platform**: Each transportation company operates independently with isolated data and personalized dashboards, but all share the same infrastructure—reducing costs and enabling quick deployment.

The result: transportation companies get actionable insights, passengers have their voices heard, and system administrators serve multiple clients efficiently from a single platform.
