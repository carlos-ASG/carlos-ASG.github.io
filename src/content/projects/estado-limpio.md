## The Problem

Citizens don't know when the garbage truck will pass through their street, resulting in accumulated trash, dirty streets, and constant complaints. Municipal authorities lack visibility over actual routes and collection service efficiency.

## The Solution

**Estado Limpio** is a platform that tracks collection trucks in real-time via GPS. Citizens can see on their smartphone when the truck will arrive at their location, while authorities have a complete administrative dashboard to supervise operations.

### How It Works

1. **For Citizens**: Open the mobile app → View map with truck locations → Check estimated arrival times → Set personalized alerts

2. **For Operators**: Trucks transmit their GPS location → System records completed routes → Dashboard shows real-time coverage

3. **For Authorities**: Web administrative panel (Vue.js) → Monitor route compliance → Identify uncovered zones → Optimize resources

### Recognition

- **InnovaTec National Finalist**: Advanced to national stage after winning local and regional rounds
- **Government Interest**: Presented to the Nayarit State Government with strong interest for potential implementation

### Technology

- **Mobile App**: Flutter for Android and iOS with interactive maps and push notifications
- **Backend**: Firebase for real-time synchronization and authentication
- **Dashboard**: Vue.js for administrative visualization and report generation

The result: a functional MVP validated in national competition demonstrating viability to improve municipal public services.
